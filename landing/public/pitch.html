One ad is a guess.
A salvo is a decision.

A batch creative factory on Genblaze + Backblaze B2: a brief fans out into N scored ad variants, every variant stored with verified provenance — and the best three ship.

The problem

One-shot generation is a gamble.

You ask a model for an ad and get one image. No batch, no comparison, no reason why it's good. Teams ship a guess and hope.

The solution

Brief in → N scored variants out → best three ship.

  • Real fan-out — one brief becomes N ad-image variants through a genuine Genblaze pipeline, run in parallel.
  • Verified provenance — every variant lands in Backblaze B2 with a manifest that's read back and hash-checked.
  • Explainable ranking — deterministic scores with a plain-English reason for every number. Same brief, same result.

Sponsor integration — the SDK is the engine

Genblaze fans out. Backblaze B2 holds the truth.

Genblaze — the pipeline
Real Pipeline.astream(max_concurrency=N) fan-out, the documented StorageBackend interface, and read_manifest(verify=True) provenance — not a for-loop of fakes.
Backblaze B2 — the store
Every variant is written through an object-storage sink with hierarchical keys campaigns/{date}/{run}/… and a manifest that verifies on read.
